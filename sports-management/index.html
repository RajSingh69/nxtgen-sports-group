A deliberate preview of the Sports Management direction currently being developed by NXT Gen.

01 / What We're Building

A developing sports-management division with the whole athlete in mind.

NXT Gen is strengthening its Sports Management side around athlete development, guidance, visibility, welfare and opportunity. The services below are in development and are presented as a clear view of the direction, not as a claim that every feature is live today.

The intended model connects individual athlete support with a managed sports-only development network for young athletes, teams and sporting communities.

02 / Two Services

Two services. One development model.

Athlete Management and NXT Gen Social Media are being developed as connected parts of one athlete-development ecosystem.

Service 02Name in Development

NXT Gen Social Media

NXT Gen Social Media is a working title for a managed, safe, sports-only development network for young athletes. It is intended to help athletes build confidence, document progress and connect around development rather than popularity.

  • Sporting profiles
  • Development journals
  • Team progress
  • Positive sporting connections
  • Development content
  • Confidence and skills
  • Online responsibility
  • Sports-only participation

03 / Safety First

A managed youth-sport concept, being built carefully.

The platform is being conceived as a managed, safety-first environment built specifically around youth sport. Safeguarding, appropriate access and responsible participation will form part of the platform's development.

At this stage, NXT Gen is not making technical or regulatory claims about systems that have not yet been designed, tested or implemented.

04 / More Than The Game

Developing the person behind the athlete.

Across both developing services, the intended NXT Gen approach is to support young athletes physically, mentally and spiritually.

Physical

Sporting development, preparation, discipline, healthy habits and long-term progression without making medical or performance guarantees.

Mental

Confidence, resilience, decision-making, communication, handling setbacks, learning and life skills through a development lens.

Spiritual

Values, purpose, character, identity, humility, respect and community, inclusive of athletes of all religions and of no religion.

06 / Global Outlook

Talent, visibility, skills and opportunity.

Designed with a global outlook, the platform aims to help young athletes and teams build visibility, skills and confidence, including within developing sporting communities in India and countries across Africa.

This is an intended direction for the developing concept, not a claim that NXT Gen currently operates across every location or community referenced.
