Our Mission

Our Mission & Values

NXT Gen Sports Group's mission is to empower the next generation of football talent by providing unparalleled opportunity and representation, and by building a truly inclusive and supportive community for our athletes.

01

Opportunity

Ensuring athletes from underrepresented backgrounds have the access, resources, and support they need to achieve their full potential and succeed in their careers.

02

Representation

Increasing the visibility and recognition of athletes from diverse communities, including South Asian players, and amplifying their voices within the sport.

03

Inclusion

Fostering a welcoming and empowering environment where all athletes, employees, and partners feel valued, respected, and have a strong sense of belonging.

04

Championing Change

We actively champion equality, diversity, and inclusion across all levels of football, challenging discriminatory practices and advocating for a more representative and equitable future for the game, with a particular focus on supporting women's football, promoting South Asian representation, and investing in grassroots initiatives.

CEO Message

A personal commitment to fairness, representation and support.

As a lifelong advocate for grassroots football and EDI (Equality, Diversity, and Inclusion), I founded NXT Gen Sports Group to create a more equitable and inclusive future for the game. I firmly believe that every athlete, regardless of their background, deserves an equal chance to succeed and reach their full potential.

Our mission at NXT Gen Sports Group is to empower the next generation of football talent by providing unparalleled opportunity and representation. We are committed to dismantling barriers and ensuring that athletes from all communities have the support and resources they need to thrive. From contract negotiations and brand building to career development, we offer comprehensive athlete management services tailored to each individual.

Beyond athlete representation, NXT Gen Sports Group is dedicated to driving positive change within football. We actively champion greater equality, diversity, and inclusion at every level of the game, from grassroots development to the professional stage. We believe that by working collaboratively with athletes, clubs, and organisations, we can build a stronger, more connected, and truly representative sporting community. My personal journey with EDI fuels my determination to be a relatable and effective advocate for athletes and to champion a world where fairness and respect are paramount.

Jagdev Singh KallhaFounder & CEO
