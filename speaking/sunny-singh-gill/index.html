01 - Biography

Who is Sunny Singh Gill?

Football is the evidence. Leadership is the lesson.

A professional football referee whose journey connects ambition, setback, pressure, representation and elite decision-making.

Sunny grew up around football and refereeing. His father, Jarnail Singh, was an important figure in English football officiating, and Sunny originally wanted to play before his own pathway shifted towards refereeing.

Scouted by QPRReferee at 15First match at 17Walked awayReturnedPremier League

As a youngster Sunny had trials with QPR after impressing in youth football. He qualified as a referee at 15, took charge of a Sunday League fixture at 17, stepped away from refereeing for several years, returned in his early twenties and progressed through the English football system.

Alongside that journey, Sunny worked as a prison officer while building his refereeing career. In March 2024, he became the first British South Asian referee to take charge of a Premier League match.

04 - Sunny's Toolkit

Principles under pressure

What has Sunny learned under pressure?

Make the decision

Football context: a referee cannot pause the match indefinitely. Workplace application: leaders often move before every variable is known.

Communicate it

Football context: people need to understand what has happened. Workplace application: clarity reduces uncertainty.

Reset quickly

Football context: the next decision arrives immediately. Workplace application: recovery is part of performance.

Listen without losing authority

Football context: emotion is part of the environment. Workplace application: listening and leadership can coexist.

Review without dwelling

Football context: improvement comes through review. Workplace application: learning should not become paralysis.
